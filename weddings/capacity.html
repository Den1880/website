Wedding Capacity And Layouts

Four rooms that work on their own or run together as one evening. Here is what each one holds, so you can stop guessing whether your list fits. Every wedding venue in Waterloo quotes one maximum capacity; this page breaks it down room by room instead, including what works for a small wedding rather than a Kitchener banquet hall.

The three questions couples ask first

“What is our real number?”

100 seated in the Theatre for a ceremony, 140 standing across the Theatre and Lobby Bar. Seated dinner with a dance floor depends on your table plan — send the guest count and you get the layout back.

“Can we use more than one room?”

That is the normal shape of a wedding here. The Theatre and Lobby Bar run as one evening, and the Library can be added for a smaller sitting.

“What if our list grows?”

Tell the events team early. The rooms combine, so the plan usually flexes without changing the date or the venue.

Room by room

What Each Space Holds

Seats up to 100

The Theatre

The ceremony room. Elevated seating in velvet, a 16-foot video wall, wireless microphones, Sonos and lighting you can set to your colours. Flexible furniture, so the same room turns over for dinner and dancing while your guests are next door.

Up to 140 with the Theatre, standing

The Lobby Bar

Full bar, ornate chandeliers, original wood, accessible washrooms and direct catering access. Where guests arrive, where cocktails happen during the turnover, and where the night tends to end.

Four 16-foot harvest tables

The Library

One long-table room with a video screen and custom seating. Right for a micro wedding, a rehearsal dinner or a shower. Available as an add-on to a Theatre rental from $1,000 per hour, or booked on its own.

A commercial kitchen on site

The Kitchen

Your caterer works from a real kitchen inside the building. It is the reason plated food arrives hot and service does not stall between courses.

How to read the numbers

Which Number You Actually Need

Venues quote one maximum. These are the five numbers that decide a wedding.

Ceremony
Up to 100 seated in the Theatre, on elevated seating so the back row still sees the vows.
Standing reception
Up to 140 across the Theatre and the Lobby Bar with the rooms open to each other.
Seated dinner
Driven by your table plan and how much dance floor you want, not by a single headline number. Send the guest count and the events team returns the layout that fits it.
A smaller sitting
The Library holds four 16-foot harvest tables — one long table rather than a room of round ones.
Combining rooms
The normal shape of a wedding here: ceremony in the Theatre, cocktails in the Lobby Bar, back into the Theatre for dinner.
